Naughty Bean Consulting started as the consulting arm of [Naughty Bean Coffee](https://www.naughtybean.coffee/), a coffee brand founded in 2024. Running a counter turned out to be an excellent way to discover that hospitality software is either expensive, foreign, or both. So we built our own point of sale. Then our own ordering platform. Then payroll, then operations, then the tools to sell them. Somewhere along the way the software became the business.

The studio is led by Shaun De Ponte, a software engineer who has shipped everything on the register, from Django monoliths that run a till to a multi-account AWS estate for an enterprise client. We stay small on purpose: the person you brief is the person who writes the code and the person who gets paged if it breaks.

## What we are for

We build software for businesses that trade in the physical world: shops, kitchens, service teams, property portfolios, payroll offices. Software that has to work on a tablet with greasy fingerprints, on a phone in a stockroom, at 6 a.m. before the first customer. Most of it is web and mobile. Some of it talks to espresso machines, biometric terminals and banks.

We are not a body shop and not an agency. We take on a small number of builds at a time and finish them.

## Where

South Africa, working remotely with clients anywhere. We quote in Rand, hold meetings in SAST and have no objection to a client's own Jira, Slack or Teams. Our own work is tracked in [AtlasHub](/products/atlashub/), which we built because we wanted one login.
