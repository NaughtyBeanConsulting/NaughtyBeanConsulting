{{ partial "section-head.html" (dict "num" "2" "title" "Scope of works" "note" "Summary — see sheet 02 for the full specification" "id" "scope") }}
  1. 1.0

    Web applications

    Customer portals, storefronts, back-office systems and the admin tooling nobody budgets for. Server-rendered by default, with just enough JavaScript to feel instant.

  2. 2.0

    Mobile apps

    Installable progressive web apps that work on a till, a barista's phone or a warehouse scanner. Offline-tolerant where the job demands it. Native builds when the brief genuinely needs the hardware.

  3. 3.0

    SaaS platforms

    Multi-tenant systems with schema-level isolation, subscription billing, custom domains with automated certificates, and role-based access that holds up under audit.

  4. 4.0

    Integrations

    Yoco, PayFast, Stripe, Paystack, iKhokha and Stitch for payments. Twilio for WhatsApp. Xero and Sage for accounting. Courier APIs, espresso-machine telemetry and biometric terminals when the job calls for them.

  5. 5.0

    Cloud & delivery

    AWS multi-account landing zones, ECS and RDS defined in CDK, GitHub Actions pipelines, and honest monitoring. Or a single well-kept VM when that is the right size.

  6. 6.0

    Security & AI features

    Penetration-test remediation, row-level tenant isolation, MFA and append-only audit trails. AI where it earns its keep: document extraction, ticket triage, order parsing over WhatsApp.
